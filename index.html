Obsidian Backup

A simple and reliable approach to backing up your Obsidian vault to Google Drive.

About the Project

Obsidian Backup uses rclone to transfer and maintain backup copies of Obsidian files in Google Drive. It is designed for personal data backup and file synchronization workflows.

How It Works

  • Local Obsidian files are stored on your computer.
  • rclone transfers files to your Google Drive.
  • Google OAuth is used to authorize Drive access.
  • Users retain control over their files and backups.
