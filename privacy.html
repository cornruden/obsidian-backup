Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Obsidian Backup, maintained by cornruden, handles information when using the rclone backup workflow.

1. Information Collection

This project website does not provide user accounts or intentionally collect personal information through its pages.

2. Google Drive Access

The backup workflow uses rclone and the Google Drive API to transfer files from a user's local computer to their Google Drive account.

Depending on the OAuth permissions selected, rclone may request access to Google Drive files. The permissions granted determine the level of access available.

3. Data Storage and Transfer

Files selected for backup are transferred from the user's computer to Google Drive through rclone. These files are stored in the user's Google Drive account and are subject to Google's privacy and security practices.

This project does not operate its own cloud storage server for users' backup files.

4. Third-Party Services

This project relies on Google Drive and rclone. Their respective privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • rclone Official Website

5. Security and Authorization

Users are responsible for protecting their Google accounts, OAuth credentials, local devices, and backup configuration files.

Users may revoke the application's Google account authorization through their Google Account settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the project or its data handling practices change.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub repository: GitHub Repository .
